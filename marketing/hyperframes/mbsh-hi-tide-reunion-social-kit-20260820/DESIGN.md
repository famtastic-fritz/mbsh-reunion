# MBSH Reunion Social Visual System

Each frame is a red-carpet invitation, not a generic announcement. The visual
world is charcoal-black with deep wine-red shadow, saturated scarlet, brushed
silver, copper-gold practical light, and the tactile contrast of velvet,
leather, metal, and film grain.

Typography is physical: Archivo Black carries the shout; EB Garamond provides a
warm editorial whisper; JetBrains Mono grounds the lower metadata strip. Copy
stays deterministic HTML so it remains correct, accessible, and easy to change.

Every cut reserves the upper-left field for the hook, uses a copper rule as an
anchored guide, and lands on an explicit MBSH registration call to action.
