D4 · .premiere-frame

Section inside a golden filmstrip frame

This whole section is wrapped in .premiere-frame. Sprocket strips on top + bottom. Side edges open to the page backdrop. The strips have a gentle gold-shimmer animation (~6s). Hover over the strips and the frame should feel like real polished tape with light catching it.

A frame is structural. It tells you "this part of the page is a scene." Six of these on the home page would be the movie-poster wall.

D4 · .premiere-divider (above and below this section)

Sections separated by golden ribbon dividers

Dividers are decorative. They thread between sections like a flowing tape. There's a subtle light-sweep that passes across the gold every ~8 seconds — barely perceptible, signals "celluloid in motion."

D8 · scroll-snap-type: y mandatory + .is-snapped bounce

Snap-into-place with bounce

The body has data-snap="on" which activates scroll-snap-type: y mandatory. Each .premiere-snap-target snaps to the top. After the snap settles, a 600ms bounce keyframe fires. Try scrolling slowly — you should feel the snap. Try scrolling fast — fast flicks pass through.

iOS Safari note: mandatory snap can fight native momentum. If the snap feels sticky on iPhone, the fallback is to flip to proximity. Test on a real device.
