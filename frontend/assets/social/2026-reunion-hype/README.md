## Creative direction

The set extends the site's established vintage-premiere language: near-black cinema spaces, crimson velvet, silver foil, warm projector light, film grain, ocean/tide imagery, and the 30+100 commemorative medallion.

The campaign has two intentional Harry treatments:

- The illustrated character remains the stable brand/cartoon treatment.
- `00-hi-tide-harry-f1-live-action-reference.png` is the previously selected F1 photoreal direction: a live-action, costumed mascot inhabiting the velvet-and-brass premiere world. Use F1—not the cartoon character sheet—as the primary reference for new cinematic photoreal campaign scenes.

`01-hi-tide-harry-ultra-real-4x5.png` is a newer polished realism experiment. It is useful for comparison, but it reads closer to premium CGI than the more tactile F1 live-action direction.

## Ultra-realism exploration

The second creative wave deliberately tests four different meanings of “real” rather than treating photorealism as one style:

1. **Living fantasy** — a biologically plausible, humanized Hi-Tide legend.
2. **Practical effects** — tactile Hollywood prosthetics and physical production materials.
3. **Premium animatronic** — a centennial machine/display character with precise mechanical detail.
4. **Mythic guardian** — an epic embodiment of the tide, legacy, and school spirit.

These are campaign experiments, not automatic replacements for F1 or the illustrated mascot. Compare audience reaction, message fit, and brand recognition before selecting a long-running direction.
