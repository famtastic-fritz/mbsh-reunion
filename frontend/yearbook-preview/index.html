How fallen Hi-Tides are handled

Memorial is not another gallery category.

01

Permission first

A separate tribute submission records relationship, permission, visibility, and a contact for corrections or removal.

02

Human review

The committee verifies names, dates, captions, media rights, and tone. Nothing publishes automatically.

03

Two viewing modes

A quiet manual tribute wall and an optional user-started memorial film with captions, pause, transcript, and no automatic sound.
